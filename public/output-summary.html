The question

Who are Costco’s ideal customers in Western Sydney — and where do we reach them?

Three audience clusters were built from location and transaction signal across Greater Sydney. The strongest is anchored on Marsden Park, and it behaves unlike a grocery audience: the trip is long, planned, repeated and chained to other big-box retail.
